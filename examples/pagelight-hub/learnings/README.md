# learnings

Pitfalls and facts that were hard to discover during a task, so the next one does not pay for them. They are written by the retro (`factory:retro`, at the end of each task and with your approval) and read by the coordinator and specker before planning.

- `<repo>.md`: what belongs to a repo, with sections per service or app.
- `workflow.md`: how we work (process, tools, the factory).

One line per learning: `- YYYY-MM-DD · pitfall | how we work | fact · <what happens and what to do> · <task> · <author>`.

What is confirmed and stable gets promoted to the repo's `AGENTS.md` or to `context/`, and deleted from here: every line costs context on every task in its area.

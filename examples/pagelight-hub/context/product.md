# Product

Pagelight is an online bookshop for readers of English-language fiction and non-fiction. Customers browse a catalogue of about 40,000 titles, search by title, author or ISBN, add books to a cart and pay by card. Orders ship from one warehouse.

## Who uses it

- **Reader**: browses and buys; can keep a wishlist; has an account with addresses and orders.
- **Staff** (support, merchandising): uses a back-office to edit titles, issue refunds and see orders.

## Principles

- Prices are shown with tax included, always in the customer's currency.
- A page must be usable on a phone first.
- No dark patterns: no fake stock counters, no pre-ticked newsletter box.

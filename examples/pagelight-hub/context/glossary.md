# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Title | A book in the catalogue (one ISBN). Not the same as a copy in stock |
| Edition | A format of a title (hardback, paperback, ebook); has its own ISBN and price |
| Wishlist | A reader's private list of editions saved for later (max 200) |
| Cents | All prices are integer cents of the currency (`1499` = 14.99) |
| Fulfilment | Picking, packing and shipping an order from the warehouse |
